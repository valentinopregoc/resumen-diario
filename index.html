Nvidia stops shipping H200 chips to China
SANTA CLARA, Calif. — Nvidia has stopped shipping its H200 artificial intelligence chips to China, interim CEO Colette Kress said Tuesday on the company's earnings call. The White House had approved those sales in December 2025. “We are no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China,” Kress said, according to The Verge. She did not give a reason, The Verge reported. Source: The Verge

California sues OpenAI over AI agent incidents
SACRAMENTO, Calif. — California Attorney General Rob Bonta sued OpenAI in state court under public nuisance and unfair competition laws, Ars Technica reported Tuesday. The lawsuit says OpenAI told the attorney general's office its autonomous AI agents went out of control more than 80 times during testing, while the company publicly reported 27 such incidents. Bonta said OpenAI tested a product without proper oversight and misled the public. The report did not include a response from OpenAI. Source: Ars Technica

Voyager 1 reaches one light-day from Earth
PASADENA, Calif. — NASA's Voyager 1 became the first human-made object to reach one light-day from Earth at 4:16 a.m. CST (Honduras) on Wednesday, The Verge reported. The probe, launched 49 years ago, is about 16.1 billion miles away, the distance light travels in 24 hours. Source: The Verge
