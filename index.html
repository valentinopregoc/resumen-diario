World

MOSCOW — Russia freed American Stephen Hubbard after more than four years in prison, AP reported. The release took place during a visit by a U.S. envoy, according to the AP headline. AP News

ST. PETERSBURG, Russia — U.S. envoy Steve Witkoff met Russian President Vladimir Putin in St. Petersburg for talks on the war in Ukraine, AP reported on Wednesday. The report did not include details of what was discussed in the information available at publication time. AP News

SEOUL, South Korea — North Korea said it will restart propaganda broadcasts along the border with South Korea, AP reported. The announcement comes as tensions between the two countries rise, according to AP. AP News

TOLEDO, Ohio — Authorities issued evacuation and shelter-in-place orders in Toledo after a chemical release, AP reported on Wednesday. The report did not list the substance or the number of people affected in the information available at publication time. AP News

BUDAPEST, Hungary — Hungarian police arrested the central bank governor from the Orbán era on suspicion of fraud and abuse, AP reported. The report did not name charges beyond that description in the information available at publication time. AP News

Tech

NEW YORK — The PHP project's maintainers said they plan to release two emergency security patches imminently and told web developers to prepare, Ars Technica reported. The Verge reported that the patches, expected Wednesday, are meant to fix two critical vulnerabilities in the programming language, which is widely used to build websites. The Verge

NEW YORK — Spotify announced Wednesday a native in-car app built directly on Google's Android Automotive operating system, The Verge reported. The app launches Wednesday in select Audi models and runs on the vehicle's infotainment system. The Verge

NEW YORK — Nvidia released GPU driver 621.01 on Wednesday, which supports Microsoft's Advanced Shader Delivery technology, The Verge reported. The technology is designed to speed up game launches and remove mid-game shader compilation, which reduces stuttering and CPU spikes. The Verge

Honduras

TEGUCIGALPA — El conflicto del gremio médico cumple 45 días con la consulta externa paralizada en hospitales y centros de salud, informó El Heraldo. El Colegio Médico convocó a sus afiliados a asambleas informativas el 21 de octubre. El Heraldo también reportó que el Ministerio Público investiga a médicos por presuntas violaciones a los derechos humanos durante las protestas. El Heraldo

TEGUCIGALPA — El Ministerio Público presentó un requerimiento fiscal contra Luis Redondo y otros 13 exintegrantes de la Comisión Permanente del Congreso Nacional, según El Heraldo. El diario indicó que esa comisión fue abolida el 9 de abril de 2026. El Heraldo

TEGUCIGALPA — Autoridades identificaron tres terrenos del Estado para construir una nueva cárcel de máxima seguridad, informó El Heraldo. La ubicación definitiva, el costo y la fuente de financiamiento aún no se han definido, según el diario. El Heraldo

TEGUCIGALPA — El Congreso Nacional analiza elevar las sanciones para adolescentes hasta 20 años o juzgar como adultos a jóvenes de 16 y 17 años por delitos graves, informó El Heraldo. El diario señaló que Honduras castiga desde los 12 años, una de las edades punibles más bajas de América Latina. El Heraldo
