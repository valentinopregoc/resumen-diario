Tech

Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra told investors Wednesday that demand for the company's memory is expected to exceed its available supply for at least the next couple of years, Ars Technica reported. Micron and Samsung executives made similar comments this week. Micron no longer sells consumer RAM; its comments refer to high-bandwidth memory for AI and DRAM for servers. Source: Ars Technica

Google began rolling out Guided Vision on Thursday in Gemini Live on compatible Android devices, The Verge reported. The feature gives real-time audio descriptions of what a phone's camera sees, such as small text or nearby objects. Source: The Verge

A federal judge dismissed two antitrust lawsuits filed by Chegg and Penske Media Corp. against Google, The Verge reported, citing Reuters. The companies had accused Google of driving away web traffic with its AI-powered search features. U.S. District Judge Amit Mehta ruled Wednesday in Google's favor. Source: The Verge

Según el informe, el programa cumplió 43.57% de las acciones previstas en 2023 y 70.72% en 2024. Source: El Heraldo

Perdomo fue condenado en 2023 por homicidio imprudente por omisión; la Sala Penal de la Corte Suprema ordenó repetir el juicio tras un recurso de casación del Ministerio Público. Source: El Heraldo

AI project lesson: Step 1 — Daily data file

Goal: make the routine save each day's news as a JSON file, so a future app page can read data instead of HTML.

Steps:

1. Open a Claude Code session on the resumen-diario repo. 2. Paste the prompt below. 3. Review the new file data/2026-10-01.json and its copy data/latest.json. 4. Open your routine and add the block under "If the routine must change".

Prompt to paste: Work directly on branch claude/resumen. Do not create another branch or a pull request. Read archivo/2026-10-01.html. Create data/2026-10-01.json with the keys world, tech, honduras, sports and lesson. Each item has title, summary, source, url, category and date. Copy it to data/latest.json. Validate both files with python3 -m json.tool, then commit and push.

If the routine must change: in step B3, add: "Also write data/AAAA-MM-DD.json and data/latest.json with the keys world, tech, honduras, sports and lesson; each item has title, summary, source, url, category (world, tech, honduras, sports or lesson) and date. Validate with python3 -m json.tool before committing."

Done when: data/2026-10-01.json exists and passes json.tool; data/latest.json has the same content.
