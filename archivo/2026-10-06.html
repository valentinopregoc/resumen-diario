World

Norway moves toward temporary smart glasses ban
OSLO — Norway is seeking a temporary ban on smart glasses, citing surveillance and privacy concerns, The Associated Press reported. The move puts the wearable devices under scrutiny over how they can be used to record people. Source: The Associated Press

Nobel medicine prize goes to three scientists
STOCKHOLM — The Nobel Prize in physiology or medicine was awarded to three scientists for work that used light to shed light on brain activity, The Associated Press reported. The research relates to understanding neural mechanisms and optogenetics. Source: The Associated Press

Spain's prime minister calls snap election
MADRID — Spain's prime minister called a snap election, The Associated Press reported. The AP said the aim is to seize momentum over the country's housing crisis. Source: The Associated Press

FBI arrests California woman in China spying case
WASHINGTON — The FBI arrested a California woman accused of spying for China on the family of Taiwan's leader, The Associated Press reported. Federal authorities have charged her with espionage-related offenses, the AP said. Source: The Associated Press

Medvedev disqualified from China Open
BEIJING — Daniil Medvedev was disqualified from the China Open for hitting a spectator with a ball during his semifinal against Novak Djokovic, The Associated Press reported. Source: The Associated Press

Tech

Google, Constellation Energy sign 3.6-GW power deal
NEW YORK — Google has entered a 3.6-gigawatt power deal with Constellation Energy, Reuters reported Tuesday. The headline did not include further terms. Source: Reuters

UK watchdog opens first Online Safety Act probe into Meta
LONDON — A British regulator opened its first investigation of Meta under the Online Safety Act, Reuters reported Tuesday. Source: Reuters

France's Mistral announces new AI model
PARIS — French artificial intelligence company Mistral announced a new AI model, Reuters reported Tuesday. Source: Reuters

Honduras

Taxistas de La Ceiba protestan por el alza de combustibles
LA CEIBA — Taxistas de La Ceiba protestaron por el alza en los combustibles, informó La Prensa. Miembros de Ataxu detuvieron operaciones sin bloquear el tránsito, pero advirtieron que bloquearán calles si el gobierno no atiende sus demandas, según el medio. Fuente: La Prensa

Médicos cumplen 29 días sin consultas por salarios atrasados
TEGUCIGALPA — Médicos hondureños cumplen 29 días sin atender consultas por salarios atrasados, informó La Prensa. Fuente: La Prensa

Más de 33,000 agentes resguardarán la Semana Morazánica
TEGUCIGALPA — Más de 33,000 agentes resguardarán la Semana Morazánica en Honduras, informó La Prensa. Fuente: La Prensa

Sesal aclara que no usa medicamentos vencidos en pacientes
TEGUCIGALPA — La Secretaría de Salud (Sesal) aclaró que los medicamentos vencidos no se usan en pacientes, informó La Prensa. El medio también reportó el hallazgo de medicamentos vencidos y equipo médico de la Sesal a la intemperie en Tegucigalpa. Fuente: La Prensa
